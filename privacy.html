Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

The short version

The tools on this site run entirely in your browser. Numbers you generate, and any IMEI you paste into the validator, are never transmitted to us and are never stored. We operate no user accounts and no database of visitors. The only third party that receives data about your visit is our advertising provider, and in regions where consent is required you are asked before that happens.

Who is responsible

This site is operated by dummy4u.com ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, or to exercise a right described below, write to support@dummy4u.com.

Information you give us

None, unless you email us. The generator and validator do all of their work locally in your browser using JavaScript. There is no form submission, no API call, and no server-side component that could receive what you type. Closing the tab discards everything.

If you do email us, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to deal with it.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, ours is delivered by a web server that records standard request logs: your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser's user-agent string, and the referring page. These logs are used to keep the site available and to investigate abuse or errors. They are retained for a short period and then deleted, and are not used to build a profile of you or combined with any other source.

Cookies and local storage

The tools themselves set no cookies and write nothing to local storage. Cookies on this site come from two sources:

  • Advertising cookies, set by our advertising provider and its partners, described in the next section.
  • A consent record, where applicable, storing the advertising choices you have made so you are not asked repeatedly.

Advertising

This site displays advertising supplied by Google, through Google AdSense. That arrangement means the following, and you should read it as a description of what actually happens rather than boilerplate:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from many other vendors at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com.
  • Third-party vendors that serve advertising here operate under their own privacy policies, which govern their use of the data they collect.

Advertising is the only reason any third party learns about your visit. We do not run analytics, social widgets, embedded fonts, or any other external script.

Visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

Before any advertising cookie is set, you are shown a consent message and asked to make a choice. Personalised advertising is used only where you have consented to it; if you decline, non-personalised advertising is served instead, which still requires the limited processing necessary to deliver and measure an ad. You may change or withdraw your choice at any time using the privacy or consent link presented with the advertising, and your decision is recorded so that it persists between visits.

Visitors in California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. Serving personalised advertising may nonetheless count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA. You can prevent it using the Google Ads Settings link above, or by using a browser that transmits the Global Privacy Control signal, which our advertising provider honours where legally required.

Children

This site is a developer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, or under 16 where local law sets that threshold. If you believe a child has provided us information by emailing us, tell us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data about you, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we do not operate accounts and hold nothing beyond short-lived server logs and any email you have sent us, most requests are straightforward to answer. Write to support@dummy4u.com and we will respond within the period your local law requires.

Requests concerning advertising data held by Google are best directed to Google, whose privacy policy is published at policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Continued use of the site after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
